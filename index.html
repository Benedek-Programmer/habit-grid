How Intensity Levels & Streaks Work

Every square on your 52-week grid represents a calendar day. Clicking a square cycles through Levels 0 to 4 so you can track both consistency and effort-whether you did a light 10-minute session (Level 1) or went all-in (Level 4).

  • Daily Streaks: Any logged level (1-4) counts toward your active daily streak. If you haven't logged today yet your streak stays alive from yesterday until midnight.
  • Custom Level Targets & Weekly Goals: Click any habit's title (or its Details button) to define what Levels 1-4 mean for that habit and set a rest-day-friendly weekly goal (e.g., 4x/week).
  • Square Notes: Right-click (or long-press on touchscreen) any past or current square to attach a short journal note. Squares with notes display a subtle and show your note on hover.
  • Drag, Archive & Share: Drag habit cards by their ⋮⋮ handle to prioritize them, archive completed habits without losing history, or download a retina Share PNG snapshot.

100% Local First Privacy

HabitGrid has no accounts, no backend database, no analytics trackers, and zero network requests. All of your habits, streaks, custom level definitions, and notes are sorted exclusively in your browser's localStorage.

Because your data lives locally on your device, use the Export button in the top-right header anytime to download a portable .json backup file that you can restore on any browser via Import.
